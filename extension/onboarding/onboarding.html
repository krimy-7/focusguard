FocusGuard AI

FocusGuard AI waits, then nudges you gently back to what you meant to do.

Looks at, in this browser
The site name and title of your current tab, when you switch tabs, and whether you’re at the computer. It skips banking, health, password and government sites, incognito windows and Chrome’s own pages.
Never leaves your browser
Page titles, full web addresses, tabs to reopen after a break, break notes, any AI key, and the moment-by-moment record of what you did.
Sent only after you sign in
Your goals, task names, the names of sites in each (like github.com, never full addresses), settings and daily totals. Over HTTPS, never sold, no ads. Export or delete it all from the dashboard. Read the privacy policy.
Optional AI helpOff
AI help

Recognises sites the built-in list doesn’t know and suggests task names. A local model or your own key can be added in Settings.
