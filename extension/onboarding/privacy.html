FocusGuard AI privacy policy

What FocusGuard AI looks at

Only after you click Start on the setup page: the site name and title of the tab you're on, when you switch tabs, and whether you're at the computer. It skips banking, health, password-manager and government sites, incognito windows, Chrome's own pages and any site you add in Settings.

What never leaves your browser

Page titles, full web addresses, the tabs to reopen after a break, your break notes, any AI key, and the moment-by-moment record of what you did.

What is sent to FocusGuard AI's server, only after you sign in

Your goals; your context names, their kind and state, and the names of sites in each context (like github.com, never full addresses); your settings; and daily totals such as focus minutes, nudges and breaks. Everything is sent over HTTPS. It is not sold and not used for ads.

Optional AI help

Off unless you turn it on. The on-device option keeps everything in your browser. If you connect a local model or your own API key, the site name, cleaned-up page title, context name and today's goal titles go only to the address you choose, and your key goes only to that address.

Your control

You can export or delete everything FocusGuard AI's server holds from the dashboard. Removing the extension deletes what it kept in this browser.
